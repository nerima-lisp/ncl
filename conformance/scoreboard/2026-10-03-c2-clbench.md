## Reproduction

```text
conformance/cl-bench/ncl-driver.sh target/release/ncl
exit 139: segmentation fault during ncl-driver.lisp loading
```

The first source-level blockers observed before the driver crash were:

- `MULTIPLE-VALUE-SETQ` and `SHIFTF` were not registered as native macro expanders. c2 added both compiler-side expanders; `files/bignum.lisp` then loaded successfully.
- `files/boehm-gc.lisp` next failed with `object error: TypeError`, at the existing `DEFSTRUCT`/CLOS path owned outside c2.
- The complete driver still crashes before registering benchmark timings, so no benchmark timing is reported as completed.

SBCL comparison was not re-measured in this run. The repository baseline is `conformance/baselines/cl-bench-sbcl-2.6.0.md`.
